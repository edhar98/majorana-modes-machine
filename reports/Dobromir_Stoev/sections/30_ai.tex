\section{On the AI collaboration}
\label{sec:ai}

\ai{A large-language model was used to organize this first draft, condense the
Block-4 notes, and check that the numerical claims match \texttt{block4.py}.  It
also helped separate frozen-state, circuit-level, parameter-level, and
optimizer-level noise so that results from different experiments were not
silently combined.  The main correction during the AI-assisted review was to
reject the tempting statement that noise-aware VQE ``recovers'' the topological
signal: the independent noiseless-fidelity check shows that a non-unital channel
can improve the displayed edge string while producing a worse state.  All
AI-drafted prose remains marked in purple and should be revised by the author
before submission.}
