\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The 1D Kitaev chain hosts unpaired Majorana zero modes at its ends in the
topological phase~\cite{kitaev2001}.
In the preceding part of the project, a parity-constrained variational
quantum eigensolver (VQE) prepared the finite-chain ground state and measured the
non-local edge string $O_{\mathrm{edge}}=X_0Z_1\cdots Z_{L-2}X_{L-1}$.  That
ideal result does not yet answer the experimentally relevant question: does the
diagnostic remain visible after imperfect gates, decoherence, readout errors,
and a noisy variational loop?

This report studies \emph{NISQ Noise on the Diagnostic}.  The first test kept
the validated state frozen and isolated readout error, local depolarization, and
finite measurement counts.  The next test applied noise during state
preparation, where it accumulates after individual gates.  Finally, the VQE
optimizer itself received noisy cost values, and a recorded backend calibration
was compared with the uniform test model.  Throughout, the same $L=4$ circuit
and edge-string observable are used so that the source of each change remains
clear.

The noiseless construction of the VQE and string-measurement circuit belongs
to Zh.\ Liu's report, \emph{Measuring Topology on Circuits}.  The
symmetry-based classification of physical Kitaev-chain errors and the scaling to
large $L$ are treated in Ed.\ Harutyunyan's report, \emph{Error Taxonomy and
Scaling to Large $L$}.  Here $T_1/T_2$ enter only as device-level channels acting
on the simulated circuit.
