\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The 1D Kitaev chain hosts unpaired Majorana zero modes at its ends in the
topological phase~\cite{kitaev2001}.
In the preceding part of the project, a parity-constrained variational
quantum eigensolver (VQE) method prepared the finite-chain ground state and measured the
non-local edge string $O_{\mathrm{edge}}=X_0Z_1\cdots Z_{L-2}X_{L-1}$.  However, that
ideal result does not yet answer the practical question of whether the
diagnostic remains visible after imperfect gates, decoherence, readout errors,
and a noisy variational loop.

This report concerns itself with \emph{NISQ Noise on the Diagnostic}.  The first test we performed kept
the validated state frozen and focused on determining readout error, local depolarization, and
finite measurement counts.  The next test applied noise during state
preparation, which accumulates after each consecutive gate.  Finally, the VQE
optimizer itself received noisy cost values, and a recorded backend calibration
was compared with the uniform test model.  Throughout the experiments, the same $L=4$ circuit
and edge-string observable were used so that the source of each change remains
clear.

The noiseless construction of the VQE and string-measurement circuit belongs
to Zh.\ Liu's report, \emph{Measuring Topology on Circuits}.  The
symmetry-based classification of physical Kitaev-chain errors and the scaling to
large $L$ are treated in Ed.\ Harutyunyan's report, \emph{Error Taxonomy and
Scaling to Large $L$}.
