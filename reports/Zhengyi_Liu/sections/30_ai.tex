\section{On the AI collaboration}
\label{sec:ai}

\ai{Large language models were used as an explanatory and auditing tool for this
part of the project.  They helped organize the VQE-to-measurement workflow,
compare the weekly notes with the implementation, and improve the presentation
of the parity and shot-estimator arguments.  Their suggestions were checked
against \texttt{block3\_core.py}, the generated figures, and exact-diagonalization
benchmarks.  Two corrections were especially important: the edge string is a
finite-size boundary diagnostic rather than a standalone bulk invariant, and the
restart ranking in the depth/sweep benchmarks is ED-assisted.  The numerical
curves themselves come from the project code, not from a language model.}
