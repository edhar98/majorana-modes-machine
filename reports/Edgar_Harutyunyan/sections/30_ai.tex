\section{On the LLM collaboration}
\label{sec:ai}

To keep this report compact, we avoid repeating material available on the
\href{https://edhar98.github.io/majorana-modes-machine/llm-feedback.html}
{LLM-feedback project page}. Instead, Figure~\ref{fig:ai-self-correction}
illustrates a representative correction made during the collaboration. The
model initially inferred that the required ansatz depth increased with $L$.
After receiving the extended cluster results for $L=4,\ldots,16$, it revised
this claim: $r^\ast=4$ remained sufficient over the tested range, while the
large-$L$ decay arose primarily from the growing CNOT count. It also identified
the anomalous high-depth points as optimisation failures rather than physical
effects.
