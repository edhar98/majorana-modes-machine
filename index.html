What the project answers

The central question is not whether Majorana physics is beautiful in the exact model. It is whether the measurable topological signal can still be prepared, verified, and read out after realistic circuit noise enters.

  • Topology gives the target. The Kitaev chain has a sharp bulk transition and exponentially split edge modes.
  • The measured signal is non-local. The useful qubit diagnostic is an edge string, not a local order parameter.
  • Noise sets the operating point. The best circuit is the shallowest one that is already expressive enough.
